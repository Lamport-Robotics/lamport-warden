    What you just saw

    Warden sits in front of the wallet. A request is read before the wallet is asked, by rules that run in your browser, and the sentence you saw is written from what the request does, not from what the site says about it.

    It remembers who you have paid, per wallet, in your browser and nowhere else. That is why the second payment went through without a word and the look-alike did not. Warden kept that for the practice wallet too; its settings page can forget it.

    Warden is a second pair of eyes, not a guarantee. A page can still reach a wallet in ways an extension never sees.
