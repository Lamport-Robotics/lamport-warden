What they do, and what they do not

Warden reads a signing request in your browser before your wallet is asked, and says what it would do. Sentinel watches the wallets you add after you sign, and tells you when something looks wrong.

  • They are a second pair of eyes, not a guarantee. We do not promise that Warden will stop every harmful request, or that Sentinel will notice every theft or notice it in time. A page can reach a wallet in ways an extension never sees, and a wallet can be emptied in one transaction.
  • You decide what to sign. What happens to your money because of a transaction you signed, or one that someone signed with your keys, is not something we can undo or make good.
  • They never hold your keys or your money. Your keys stay in your wallet. Sentinel can move nothing of yours unless you set a wallet up for rescue, and then only the tokens you set up, and only into the Lamport Vault you named. Rescue has its own terms, below, and they are shown again before you can turn it on.
  • Nothing here is financial, investment or legal advice.

Your part

  • Keep your keys to yourself, and the addresses of your alert channels too: on ntfy, whoever knows a topic's name can read what is sent to it.
  • Add only wallets that are yours. Sentinel asks each one for a signature before it is added.
  • Do not try to break the service, overload it, get around its limits, or resell it.

The paid plan

Watch is free. Pro is paid by the month, by card, through Stripe; or, where the Sentinel page offers it, paid ahead in USDC or SOL.

  • $15 is charged to your card today, and again every month until you cancel. Stripe takes the payment; Sentinel never sees the card.
  • Cancel any time from this page. The plan then runs to the end of the month you paid for, your profile goes back to Watch, and nothing is deleted.
  • The price follows what this profile's wallets hold: $10 a month while they hold 250,000 $LAMPORT between them, $15 otherwise. It is checked every day, a change applies from your next bill, and your alert channels are told before that bill.

"This page" in those lines is the Sentinel page, where the same words are shown before you pay. If the amount shown there differs from the one here, the one shown there is what you are charged.

Paid ahead in USDC or SOL, for one month here; three and twelve months are the same at three and twelve times the price:

  • $15 pays for one month, once. Nothing is charged again and nothing renews: when the time is up your profile goes back to Watch, and nothing is deleted. Your alert channels are told three days before.

  • Paying again before the time is up adds to it. A refund is on the same terms as by card, and is sent back to the wallet that paid.

A payment counts when the chain has made it final and the amount that arrived is at least the amount shown. An amount in SOL is priced when it is shown and holds for ten minutes. A payment that is short, or that arrives after a price in SOL has run out, starts nothing: write to us and it is sent back to the wallet it came from, less the network's fee.

Rescue

Rescue is part of Pro, and is there only where the Sentinel page offers it: it needs Lamport Vault and the rescue program on the network, and they are not on Solana mainnet yet. It is off until you set a wallet up for it, and these lines are shown to you before you can:

  • Setting a wallet up is your signature. It names one Lamport Vault of yours, makes a token account in that vault for each token (you pay the network's deposit for each), and gives the rescue program permission to move each token account you set up. That permission has no limit in amount, does not run out, and has, as the program stands, one destination: that vault.
  • A rescue moves every token account you set up into that vault, whole. You can start one. So can Sentinel: when you ask it to, or on its own after a serious alert if you switch that on. Whoever holds one of Lamport's rescuer keys could start one at a time you did not choose; your tokens would then be in your vault, not gone.
  • Only your vault's key moves anything out of the vault. If you lose that key, what was rescued is lost with it, and we cannot get it back.
  • A rescue does not cover SOL, tokens of a kind this wallet did not hold when you set it up (set it up again to cover them), frozen token accounts, tokens that an ordinary transfer cannot move, or anything staked, lent or listed somewhere else. A wallet can be emptied in one transaction, and a rescue can come too late.
  • The rescue program and Lamport Vault are public code that no outside firm has audited, and Lamport holds the key that can change the rescue program. You use rescue at your own risk, within the limits in these terms.
  • You can take the setup away at any time on the Sentinel page: your wallet revokes each permission and closes the setup.

A rescue that was started on a false alarm has moved your tokens into your own vault. We do not undo it, and nothing is owed for it: you move them back with your vault's key.

  • You can leave at any time: removing your last wallet on the Sentinel page removes your profile, and removing the extension removes what it kept.
